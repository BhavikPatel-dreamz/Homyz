## Approval gate

Foundation work may define schemas, outbox contracts, immutable histories, evidence capture and nonfinancial projections with all `BLOCKED` policies isolated. It must not activate discounts, points other than a policy-approved ledger path, free nights, expiry, upgrade/late-checkout promise, tier downgrade, high-quality review award, geography/language award, or referral conversion credit until the corresponding decision is approved.
