# Project Guidelines & Styling Preferences

## Typography & Font Weights
- **Font Weight Rule**: Do not make fonts `bold` (`font-bold`, `<b>`, `<strong>`) or `semibold` (`font-semibold`).
- Instead of `bold` or `semibold`, always use `font-medium` (or `font-normal` for body copy/descriptions).
- Maintain this consistent styling across all host dashboards, pages, and components.
